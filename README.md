## 🚀 About Power BI

**Microsoft Power BI** is a business intelligence and data visualization tool used to connect, transform, analyze, and visualize data. It helps turn complex datasets into interactive reports and dashboards that support data-driven decision-making.

* **DAX** – Creating measures, calculated columns, and KPIs

* **Data Visualization** – Creating clear and effective charts and reports
* **Data Analysis** – Identifying trends, patterns, and business insights
* **Dashboard Design** – Developing user-friendly and interactive dashboards
